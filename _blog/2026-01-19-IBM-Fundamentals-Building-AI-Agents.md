# My Thoughts on IBM Fundamentals of Building AI Agents

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1100/format:webp/1*tZDgONKrQHz3UW0UQSTOmw.jpeg)

Okay let's cut straight to the crux of the matter — why you're here.

You want to know if the [IBM Fundamentals of Building AI Agents](https://www.coursera.org/learn/fundamentals-of-building-ai-agents?specialization=ibm-rag-and-agentic-ai) course is worth your time, effort, and money?

I will try my very best to share with you my thoughts after enrolling and completing the course.

But before I do that, if you're curious to know what Coursera promises you'll learn, here's the [official link to the course](https://www.coursera.org/).

---

## 1. Why Did I Take This Course — and Why Should You?

I'm a data scientist and I have spent many years building AI models to solve challenges in various sectors — from healthcare, to energy, to academia and more.

And in 2022, the world was set on a frenzy. OpenAI released ChatGPT.

I was immediately assigned full responsibility to build a prototype research tool for document analysis using OpenAI's large language model API.

In that project, at the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) office in Istanbul, I experimented with LangChain's tools such as embeddings, vectorstores, chains, chat models, prompt templates and more. And that gave me a glimpse of the seismic change the world of software development was about to undergo.

After completing the project and transitioning to my new role as a Data Scientist at the [Robert Koch Institute](https://www.rki.de/DE/Home/home_node.html) in Berlin, I took a step back and watched from the sidelines how the shock waves of LLM development swept across different industries.

Now, fast-forward to 2026 — the discussion in the AI arena has shifted from using LLMs merely as text-generation and pattern recognition machines, to extending their function to interact with the real world: that is, the **Agentic AI** approach.

This is a complete game-changer and I couldn't be left behind. So I had to pick up from where I had dropped the ball and stay on top of the AI development trend.

That's why I looked around and found the [IBM RAG and Agentic AI Professional specialization](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/ibm-rag-and-agentic-ai#courses) as the best systematic path to become fluent in Agentic AI.

> If you're a data scientist looking to upskill, or an AI enthusiast who's just curious about what all the fuss around Agentic AI is about — this course can be a good first step.

---

## 2. How Much Time Does It Take to Complete the Course?

The website says 11 hours. I say it depends.

It depends on your current understanding of the Python programming language and the LangChain framework. It also depends on your motivation for taking the course.

> I wanted to learn how to build Agentic workflows from scratch. So I wasn't in any hurry to just watch videos, complete the quizzes and lab sessions, get the certificate, and scream "Hurray, I did it!!"

No.

I took my time to understand what each piece of code was doing and replicated it myself. I even went the extra mile of running the lab sessions locally, developing a user-interactive interface using Streamlit, and playing around with the agents. This takes a bit more time and patience — but it's worth it.

---

## 3. How Much Does It Cost to Enroll?

Coursera offers monthly and annual subscriptions of €50 and €342 respectively. These give you access to almost all course specializations, including the IBM RAG and Agentic AI Professional Certificate. You can also enroll for free for 7 days without charge.

Now, here's a little secret most people don't know about the platform:

**It offers 75% financial aid for almost all its courses.** Which means you get to pay only about €10 per course. So if you're low on budget, consider this option. Note that it takes about 2 weeks for financial aid to be granted.

---

## 4. What Did You Like About the Course?

- **The extra readings** provided throughout the course. They allowed me to digest the content bit by bit at my own pace.
- **The lab walkthroughs.** These provided the most value for me. I want to build Agentic workflows, so I need to practice. The lab sessions gave me the basic foundation from which I can build more complex Agentic workflows.
- The course hammered on the idea of ensuring I understand how **tools are defined and structured**, how LLMs interact with these tools, and how LangChain provides an easy framework for agents to orchestrate LLMs and tools to achieve set goals.

---

## 5. What Did You Dislike About the Course?

- The videos were too fast for me, and the voice sounded like it was AI-generated *(I might be wrong — who knows these days what's AI and what's human?)*. I'm an AI advocate but I still enjoy the human touch.

---

## 6. What Advice Do You Have for Someone About to Take the Course?

**Take notes as you read and listen to the videos. Don't just copy and paste.** Write them in your own words. This will serve as a quick memory jogger whenever you return to the content.
