The story so far

I didn’t start in AI. I started with antennas.

Growing up in Cameroon, I studied Telecommunications Engineering at the University of Buea — learning how signals travel, how systems talk to each other, how to solve hard problems from first principles. That foundation turned out to be more useful than I ever imagined.

In 2017, a course on Udacity introduced me to artificial intelligence. Something clicked. I could see AI’s potential to change everything — healthcare, climate, how we learn, how we work. I became obsessed.

What followed was a decade of relentless curiosity, crossing continents, institutions, and disciplines.
