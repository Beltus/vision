Welcome to my blog

Hi, I'm Beltus Nkwawir. I work as a Research Associate and Data Scientist at the Robert Koch Institute's Center for Artificial Intelligence in Public Health Research in Berlin Germany.

I design and build agentic AI systems powered by large language models to help people find critical and accurate health knowledge faster.

For more than a decade, I have been working in AI, Data Science, and their associated fields. But my journey didn't start in AI. It started as a Telecommunications Engineering major, where I earned my Bachelor's Degree from the University of Buea, Cameroon. It build the neccessary engineering foundations in mathematics, programming, systems thinking, understanding and solving problems from first principle. From 2017, where I was introduced to AI via the Udacity Online Platform, it sparked my passion for AI as I could see it's potential in transforming every facet of our lives. That led me to pursuing a Masters in Computer Science at Turkiye's oldest and most prestigious university, Istanbul Technical University (ITU) as a fully-funded scholar of the Turkish Government Scholarship Programme. While working in Berlin, I'm also a PhD candidate at ITU in Türkiye, where my research aims to solve two pressing challenges of in the domain of Data Centers: Soaring operational costs of Data Centers and their Equivalent Carbon Emissions. I have had the incredible opportunity of working on projects across and with multiple international organisations including United Nations Development Programme in Istanbul, and World Health Organization Hub in Berlin using AI and Data science to solve Sustainability issues, build capacity, and strengthen global pandemic and epidemic intelligence systems.
